> *That's what being welcomed feels like. That's what being celebrated for who you are feels like.* 🇯🇲

It's been exactly a month since I started at Foundation Commerce.

Looking back to last year, I probably would have booked my birthday off and made a big thing of celebrating with friends and family. This year, my birthday landed right around the time the kids broke up from school, Emancipation Day and Jamaican Independence Day.

I was born in Jamaica and grew up there, only leaving to live in the UK when I was 20. So while I'm proudly both British and Jamaican, when it comes to Jamaican Independence Day, I celebrate it entirely as a Jamaican. 🇯🇲

The funny thing is, this year I was at work on Independence Day.

## A smaller agency

Having worked in larger organisations, you get used to structure, policy and the machinery of a bigger business. Personal things can stay just that: personal.

This year was different, and it got me thinking about my first month as Head of Development at Foundation Commerce.

Being a smaller agency means you are 100% driven by the success of your clients. Your reputation matters. Attention to detail matters. The quality of what you deliver matters. **There's nowhere to hide.**

## It was the people

What really stood out this week wasn't just the work. It was the people.

After an early deployment of a new Hyvä website with the team, for one of our big-brand clients, I was exhausted. Then one of my colleagues, Ivan, checked in to see how it had gone. And there it was on his screen: 🇯🇲 a Jamaican flag.

He had changed his background to celebrate me, his teammate, on Jamaican Independence Day.

Honestly, that moment restored me. It made me smile, gave me a second wind, and became my favourite day since joining Foundation. Then, call after call, the Jamaican flag kept appearing in the background.

**That's what being welcomed feels like.** That's what being celebrated for who you are feels like.

Some things are meant to be. And perhaps the fact that Foundation's brand colour is green is just another little piece of symbolism. 🇯🇲💚

## Fighting above our weight

Foundation may be a smaller agency, but it fights well above its weight class. The expertise is clear, the ambition is obvious, and the standard we hold ourselves to is high.

Ryan has already agreed that the next summer social is in Jamaica if we hit our higher bonus targets, so we're definitely holding him to that. 😂

For now, we're focused on one thing: wowing our clients. There's something big brewing at Foundation Commerce. Watch this space.
