> *I always judge a client meeting by one thing: did we agree what "done" actually looks like before anyone touches a sprint board?*

Happy to say last week's visit to Power Tool World did.

## Turning data into priorities

We spent the day turning their customer-journey data into practical priorities, with mobile checkout friction and search and navigation at the top of the list. We reviewed their site performance, stability and security, and how to hold delivery pace through peak trading.

Then we built a clearer, ROI-led roadmap: what fits into monthly capacity, what needs to become its own standalone project, and why. There's a longer list sitting behind it too: loyalty, B2B, checkout, merchandising and AI search visibility.

**Clear priorities. Clear capacity. A shared plan for measurable impact.**

## How we try to work

That's the model we work to at Foundation Commerce: transparent planning, shared priorities, measurable outcomes. Focusing on the overall picture, not just reacting to whatever's next on the ticket queue.

The difference shows up later. When everyone agrees what done looks like at the start, nobody is surprised at the end.
